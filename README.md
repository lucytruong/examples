### A little about me:
This repository reflects over 15 years of hands-on experience in digital analytics implementation — shaped by a deep foundation in JavaScript, a strong focus on data accuracy, and a practical, strategy-first approach to event tracking.

My journey into programming began early, inspired by my brothers’ networking studies and the technical books around our home. After exploring journalism through work placements at the BBC and a local newspaper, I discovered my true interest lay in coding — particularly Java, SQL, and JavaScript — which led me to study New Media and Internet Technology and pursue an external Java course.

Professionally, I transitioned from graphic design into analytics by improving email targeting using JavaScript with CheetahMail, long before tag management systems were standard. Since then, I’ve built custom tracking solutions across various platforms and industries, including solving complex challenges like tracking Xbox streaming traffic at Channel 4 without SDK access — creating custom server-side Adobe payloads to deliver critical insights.

A turning point came while working at The Economist, where I was introduced to ***Snowplow***. Its philosophy — explicitly defining events, modeling structured data, and maintaining ownership of the tracking strategy — deeply influenced my approach. Since then, I’ve prioritized purposeful data collection that serves business goals, not just volume.

This repo is a product of that mindset: focused on ***data accuracy***, ***hygiene***, and strategic event design. It represents what I’ve found to be the most effective and scalable approach to implementation — track what matters, structure it well, and ensure every data point has a clear purpose.
